# Development State

Updated 2026-08-22. The project goal is a reproducible fixed-scene chain: Gemini RGB-D mapping/relocalization and supervised mobile navigation, followed by white-arm ACT grasping. ACT retains the 28-episode / 19,309-frame v2 dataset and checkpoint; a grasp, short transfer, and release have been observed, but repeatable success and a gripper-feedback supervisor remain unfinished. The fixed downward-Gemini SLAM candidate is `/data/slam/mapping/20260814T140025Z/rtabmap.db`, with gimbal raw ID7=4066/ID8=1924 and `configs/slam/base_to_gemini_mapping_down_20deg_candidate.yaml`; the gimbal must not move during map-based localization or navigation. Relocalization, map overlay and Nav2 `ComputePathToPose` planning work. On 2026-08-22, a roughly 0.254 m supervised path was planned, but during the rotate-only stage camera-only VO materially disagreed with physical yaw and reported false translation, so the rotation guard stopped before any forward command. Separately, the white-board wheel bus has intermittently returned `communication=-6` on all three wheel IDs; after power reseating, 90/90 low-rate and 900/900 high-rate read-only diagnostics plus three raised-wheel stop checks ultimately passed. This is not proof of reliable grounded motion or a confirmed hardware root cause. Do not continue medium/long autonomous routes, weaken the rotation guard, or enable the unverified fused stack. The next gates are: repeatable grounded short-motion/verified-stop evidence; read-only wheel feedback and Gemini IMU axis/sign calibration; then a verified wheel-translation plus IMU-yaw `odom -> base_link` source, with RTAB-Map retained as `map -> odom` correction.
